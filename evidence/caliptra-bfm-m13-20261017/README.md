## Final classification

The 44-row table includes c339, current-main pinned-only, and current-main-plus-overlays statuses. Raw c339 counts were 19 PASS, 22 BLOCKED, and 3 FAIL; correcting the two ECC zero-vector passes gives 17 PASS, 24 BLOCKED, and 3 FAIL. Current-main pinned-only is 11 PASS, 30 BLOCKED, 1 FAIL, and 2 TIMEOUT. Current-main-plus-overlays is 19 PASS, 20 BLOCKED, 2 FAIL, and 3 TIMEOUT.

Two of the current-main raw pass markers were vacuous: the Montgomery multiplier read zero vectors, and power2round had no generated vectors. Power2round becomes a valid PASS with the vector overlay. The entropy source test did run and compare 12 words despite its unused zero-valued counter. The NTT masked reduction row is also a valid PASS without the generic marker: it completed all 1,000,000 vectors, reached interval 999999, and printed no mismatches.

The two TIMEOUT rows, `masked_barrett_redux_tb` and `mlkem_masked_gs_butterfly_tb`, each reached the 6,900-second cap without a testcase result. `ntt_masked_mult_reduction_tb` finished the full million-vector loop in 6,682 seconds with zero mismatches.

The existing setup overlays add six valid PASS results: CSRNG, power2round, sigdecode_h, norm_check, skdecode, and decompose. The pkdecode source-base correction and five-vector ECC Montgomery rerun also pass. The ECC-top rerun used ten valid vectors and reached its 6,900-second cap after TC0–TC3 completed all four operations; TC4 keygen had started, with no mismatch markers. Its generic result parser does not recognize the testcase-specific success messages, so the row is classified TIMEOUT rather than PASS. The generated-vector overlay exposes a testbench/generator failure in rej_bounded; sigencode_z produces all-X comparisons from a testbench/RTL call-shape mismatch; compress still has stale testbench ports after its filelist fix.

The c339 simulator defects found in the 44-row baseline already have minimized issues in `dsellerbrock/iverilog-uvm`, both labeled `from-caliptra-dv`: [#538](https://github.com/dsellerbrock/iverilog-uvm/issues/538) covers the VVP unpacked-array flag limit, and [#539](https://github.com/dsellerbrock/iverilog-uvm/issues/539) covers packed-struct assignment-pattern member sizing. Both are closed. No additional simulator defect is confirmed on current main: the #539 reproducer passes there, and the `el2` interface continuous-assignment errors remain unconfirmed. The pkdecode mismatch before its source-base correction came from the testbench writing vectors to an unconnected source address. No upstream issue was opened or changed.
